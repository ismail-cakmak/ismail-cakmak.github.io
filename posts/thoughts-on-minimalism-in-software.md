---
title: Thoughts on Minimalism in Software
date: 2026-02-28
tags: software
excerpt: Simplicity is not about having fewer files. It is about making the right things obvious.
---

Minimalism in software is often confused with reduction. People remove features, delete abstractions, or compress code and call the result simpler. Sometimes it is. Often it just becomes harder to understand.

The version of minimalism I care about is structural. It means the system has a small number of concepts, each concept has a clear boundary, and the common path is easy to trace without mental gymnastics.

> Simplicity is a property of understanding, not only of implementation size.

That is why a straightforward script can be more maintainable than a flexible framework layer, even if the framework looks cleaner in isolation.

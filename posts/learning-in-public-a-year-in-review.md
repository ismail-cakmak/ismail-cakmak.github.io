---
title: Learning in Public: A Year in Review
date: 2026-01-10
tags: personal
excerpt: The biggest gains came from writing down what I was confused about before I solved it.
---

This year I got more value from small public notes than from any polished long-form post. Short writeups forced me to turn vague intuition into concrete language, and that exposed the gaps in my thinking much faster.

A useful pattern emerged. Whenever I felt stuck, I wrote three things:

- What I believed
- What evidence I had
- What would prove me wrong

That habit made debugging easier, improved technical conversations, and gave me a record of how my understanding changed over time.
